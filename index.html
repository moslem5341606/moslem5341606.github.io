Postdoctoral researcher · Energy systems

Moslem Uddin

Community microgrids, intelligent energy management and sustainable power systems

I study how local energy systems can become more affordable, reliable and climate conscious. My research connects microgrid design with operational decisions: integrating renewables and storage, coordinating dispatch, and understanding tradeoffs among cost, emissions and resilience.

PublicationsCVContact

Research focus

Intelligent community microgrids

  • Design and operation: integrated solar, wind, battery, diesel and grid systems under Australian operating conditions.
  • Energy management: supervisory control, price-responsive battery scheduling, peak shaving and economic dispatch.
  • Reliability and impact: outage resilience, emissions and cost–reliability tradeoffs across operating scenarios.

Emerging directions

  • Learning-based energy management and hydrogen storage.
  • Empirical assessment of community microgrids across Asia and the Pacific.
