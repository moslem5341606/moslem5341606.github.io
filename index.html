Research Associate · UNSW Canberra

Dr Moslem Uddin

Community microgrids · Intelligent energy management · Power systems engineering

I research the design and operation of reliable, affordable, low-emission energy systems. My work combines renewable integration, storage, power system simulation and intelligent control for community microgrids.

PublicationsCVGoogle Scholar ↗

Research focus

  • Community microgrids: planning and operation of solar, wind, storage, conventional generation and grid-connected systems.
  • Energy management: supervisory control, price-responsive dispatch, peak shaving, optimization and reinforcement learning.
  • Power system studies: modelling, reliability, resilience, economic performance and emissions.
