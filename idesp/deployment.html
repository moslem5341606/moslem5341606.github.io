Deployment Science

From successful pilots to repeatable deployment

IDESP studies why technically viable decentralized-energy systems do—or do not—scale in practice.

CMDRF

Community Microgrid Deployment Readiness Framework

A structured framework for assessing technical, economic, regulatory, institutional, social and replication readiness.

Why deployment science?

Engineering studies often establish whether a system can work. Deployment science examines the additional conditions required for it to become bankable, repeatable and scalable.

DimensionExamples
TechnicalPerformance, interoperability, reliability, resilience
EconomicCAPEX, OPEX, financing, business models
RegulatoryMarket access, tariffs, network rules, approvals
InstitutionalGovernance, ownership, capability, accountability
SocialParticipation, trust, equity, local value
ReplicationStandardization, modularity, transferability, scale
