Deployment science

Understanding why technically viable systems do—or do not—scale

IDESP extends conventional engineering analysis to the institutional, financial, regulatory and community conditions that shape real deployment.

Community Microgrid Deployment Readiness Framework

CMDRF provides a structured way to assess readiness across technical, economic, regulatory, institutional, social and replication dimensions, connecting pilot evidence to scalable deployment pathways.

  • Technical and operational readiness
  • Economic and financing readiness
  • Regulatory and market readiness
  • Institutional and governance readiness
  • Community and social readiness
  • Replication and scale readiness

Why it matters

Many decentralized-energy demonstrations prove that technology can work. Deployment science asks the next question: what conditions allow successful demonstrations to become repeatable, bankable and scalable infrastructure?
