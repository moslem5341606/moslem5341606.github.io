Platform

A common research stack from raw evidence to deployable systems

IDESP is designed as durable research infrastructure rather than a collection of isolated studies.

1 · Data Demand, weather, prices, assets, outages, projects, settlements and market evidence.
2 · Component models PV, wind, BESS, diesel, hydrogen, grid, network and multi-energy systems.
3 · Intelligence Supervisory EMS, MILP, metaheuristics, RL/DRL and uncertainty methods.
4 · Simulation & digital twins Annual simulation, scenarios, resilience studies and co-simulation.
5 · Deployment science Readiness, regulation, finance, institutions and community context.
6 · Outputs KPIs, figures, datasets, software, papers, dashboards and decision support.
