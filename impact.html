From research to system outcomes

Research influence

My publications examine microgrid design and operation, peak-load management, energy storage, renewable integration and decision methods. More recent work considers AI-enabled forecasting and energy management. These connected topics support the wider research discussion on how local electricity systems can be assessed and controlled.

Practical relevance

These methods are relevant to utilities, energy communities and future electricity networks exploring:

  • peak-demand reduction and lower operating cost;
  • higher use of renewable generation and storage;
  • resilience during disruptions and lower-carbon operation;
  • community-scale system planning and control.

These are applications and research objectives, not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
